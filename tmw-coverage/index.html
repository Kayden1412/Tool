How it works

05
1

Export from Anki

Select your deck → Export → Notes in Plain Text. Keep the field separator as tab. Column headers are fine — they’re skipped automatically.

2

Drop the file

The export is parsed locally. If column autodetection misses, pick the expression and reading columns yourself. Nothing is sent to a server.

3

Read the coverage

See the share of unique expressions already in TMW, how deep into each frequency tier you’ve mined, and a searchable card-by-card report.

Why do some words show as missing? TMW is a frequency list, not a complete dictionary. Kana-only cards, inflected verbs and names may not have an exact headword match — that’s what the reading fallback and the CSV export are for.

Privacy: the dictionary files and your export are read with browser APIs only. No network requests carry your deck.
